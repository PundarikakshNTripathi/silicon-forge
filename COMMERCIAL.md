# Commercial licensing

SiliconForge is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). For most uses
that is all you need:

- Running it, internally or in a company, on your own designs is allowed.
- Your RTL, constraints, programs, layouts and reports are yours. Running SiliconForge on a design does not make the
  design AGPL.
- If you modify SiliconForge and let other people use your modified version over a network (for example a hosted
  service built on the web cockpit or the API), section 13 of the AGPL requires you to offer those users the source
  of your modified version. If you distribute modified copies, the usual GPL terms apply.

A separate commercial license exists for organisations that cannot work within those terms, for example because they
want to embed SiliconForge in a closed product or run a modified hosted version without publishing the changes.
It covers the same code; there is no feature split between the two.

To ask about a commercial license, open an issue titled "Commercial license" on the repository. The maintainer will
reply there or move the conversation elsewhere.

Contributions are accepted under the grant described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), which is what makes a
commercial license possible.
